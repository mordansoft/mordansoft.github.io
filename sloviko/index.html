Sloviko

Learn words, one right answer at a time.

A minimal vocabulary trainer. Each card shows a word — you pick the right translation from six options. Get it right and the word is marked learned. That is the whole app.

// What it does

Built to stay simple

One task per screen

See a word, pick its translation from six choices. Correct answers mark the word learned — no menus in the way.

Works fully offline

The whole word set lives on the device. Study on a plane, on the metro, with the network off.

Progress you can see

A quiet counter tracks how many words you have learned out of the set. Facts, not streak pressure.

No account required

Open it and start. No sign-up, no email, no permissions the app does not need.

No catch. No subscription.

Sloviko is a small, focused app that does one thing. The words are yours, the data stays on your phone, and there is nothing to cancel.
