// Signature service

App data review.

Stop guessing. Read the data.

Most small apps are steered by gut feel, because the numbers live in four dashboards that never talk to each other. We pull your Google Play Console, App Store Connect, subscription service and analytics into one dataset, and hand back a written report: where installs, trials and paying users drop off, and what each drop costs you per month.

  • ◆One dataset from every source your app already has
  • ◆A report with the money attached to each drop-off point
  • ◆A ranked action list, most valuable first

We report what the numbers say. We don't promise growth — that depends on what you do with the findings.
