Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Bodyladder has no sign-up and no ads. Your level, workouts, and history stay on your device and work offline.

What is free and what is paid?

Push-ups and pull-ups — two full branches, ten steps each — are free forever. Core, legs, and handstand open with the full ladder: a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Every step of every branch can be read without paying; the paid part is training them.

How do I cancel the subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bodyladder → Cancel. On Android: Play Store → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Bodyladder → Cancel. Cancelling stops the renewal; the ladder stays open until the end of the paid period, and the free branches stay free.

I paid, but the branches are locked

Open Settings → Restore purchase, with the store account you paid from. If it still shows as locked, write to us with the receipt from Apple or Google and we will sort it out.

How does a step pass?

A step passes when you hit its target — all working sets at or above the number — in two workouts in a row. Anything lower keeps you on the same step. You can also move up or down by hand: the check is a guide, not a gate.

The check put me too high or too low

Open the step you want on the ladder and move there by hand. The check never places you higher than the answer it was given, and going back a step costs nothing.

Where is my progress stored? Can I move it to a new phone?

On your device only. Settings → Export to file saves everything in one file; on the new phone, Settings → Import from file replaces the data there with it. Deleting the app deletes the data with it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app — everything stored on the device goes with it. Purchase records held by our payment provider are deleted on request: write to mordansoft@gmail.com.

Is it safe to train from an app?

Bodyladder is a training guide, not medical advice, and it does not replace a doctor or a coach. If you have an injury, a heart, blood pressure, or joint condition, if you are pregnant or have recently given birth, check with a doctor before you start. Stop if you feel sharp pain, chest pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath that does not pass with rest. The full note is in the app: Settings → Safety.
