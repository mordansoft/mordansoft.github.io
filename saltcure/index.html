SaltCure

Make bacon, pancetta, biltong, jerky and salami at home — without guessing the salt.

SaltCure is a meat curing calculator and batch tracker in one: it works out your cure to the gram, tracks every batch from day one to ready, and walks a first-timer through their first bacon without special gear.

// What's inside

Three things it does

Dry and wet cure calculator

Equilibrium-percentage dosing for both methods, in grams or ounces, °C or °F. Change the meat weight and every figure follows.

Your nitrite standard, checked

Cure #1, Cure #2, peklosol, NPS or your own percentage — the dose is checked against USDA/FSIS limits. American calculators assume one standard; this one does not.

A log for every batch

Days and reminders, weight loss tracked against the target band, photos and a rating when it is done.

Recipes to start from

First-timer recipes with difficulty and timing, so a first bacon does not need special gear or a forum thread.
