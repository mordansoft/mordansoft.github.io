SaltCure — Meat Curing Tracker

Cure calculator & batch tracker for bacon, biltong, jerky, salami and pancetta.

What it does

SaltCure is a pocket meat-curing assistant. It calculates your cure to the gram, tracks every batch from day one to ready, and walks first-timers through their first bacon.

  • Cure calculator — dry cure and brine, salt, sugar and curing salt for the exact weight of your meat
  • Equilibrium curing by percentage, with nitrite kept inside USDA/FSIS reference limits
  • Batch tracker with step reminders — never miss a flip, rinse or equalize day
  • Weight-loss tracker shows the % lost and when a batch is ready
  • Beginner guides, starting with "Your First Bacon"
  • Offline-first — your batches live on your device, no account required
