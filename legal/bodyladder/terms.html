Your health and safety

Exercise carries a risk of injury. Please read the Safety section in the app before you start. In short:

  • Check with a doctor before you start if you have an injury, a heart, blood pressure or joint condition, if you are pregnant or have recently given birth, or if you are unsure whether exercise is safe for you.
  • Stop the exercise if you feel sharp or shooting pain, chest pain, dizziness or shortness of breath that does not pass with rest.
  • You decide which step to train, how hard and how often. Move on only when the current step feels controlled, warm up first, train on a safe surface with space around you, and use equipment such as a bar or rings only if it is sound and properly mounted.
