Privacy Policy

Bodyladder is a calisthenics skill-tree app developed by MordanSoft. It guides you through bodyweight exercises step by step and keeps a log of your training. This policy explains what happens to your data — and the short answer is: your training stays on your device, and what leaves it is anonymous usage statistics, crash reports and, if you buy Bodyladder Pro, an anonymous record of that purchase.

No account, no servers of ours

Bodyladder has no accounts and no sign-up, and we run no server that stores your data. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, body measurements or health information, and we have no way to identify you personally.

Your training data

Your answers to the starting-level questions, the steps you have reached, the sets, reps and hold times you log, and your training history are stored in a local database on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and they are not sent to our analytics. Bodyladder does not read or write Apple Health, Health Connect or Google Fit, and it does not use your device’s motion sensors, camera or location.

If you use Export, a file with your progress is created and goes wherever you send it — that file is yours, and it does not pass through us. Import reads a file you choose and keeps it on your device.

Usage statistics and crash reports

Released versions of Bodyladder send anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase and crash diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics (both provided by Google). Apart from the purchase records described below, this is the only data that leaves your device, and we use it for one purpose: to see which parts of the app work and which fail, so we can fix them.

What is sent: that a screen or step in the app happened (for example: the app was opened for the first time, the starting-level questions were completed or skipped, a step was opened, a workout was started or finished, a step was completed, a locked branch was tapped, the purchase screen was shown, a purchase or restore happened), which branch and step that was, your device model, operating system version, app version, language and country, and a random app-instance identifier generated on your device. Crash reports add the technical details of the failure.

What is never sent: your answers to the starting-level questions, the number of reps, sets or seconds you log, your training history, your name, email or contacts, your exact location, and any advertising identifier.

Development builds send nothing at all. Google processes this data as our service provider, on servers that may be outside your country, including in the United States, under the transfer safeguards in Google’s own terms. Anonymous event data is retained for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days. We cannot link any of it back to you, and we do not try to.

No advertising, no tracking

Bodyladder shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDKs. We do not profile you, we do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not use advertising identifiers, and we never sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

When the app uses the internet

Only for the statistics, crash reports and purchase checks described in this policy. The exercises, steps and illustrations are built into the app, and every part of your training works without a connection.

Purchases: Bodyladder Pro

Two branches of Bodyladder are free, and Bodyladder Pro is an optional upgrade that unlocks the other branches, sold as a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase. It is sold and charged by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under that store’s own terms. We never see your card details, your billing address or your store account name.

To know whether Pro is active on your device, Bodyladder uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States) as our service provider. When the app starts and when you buy or restore, RevenueCat receives a random identifier that it creates for your installation, the receipt or purchase token from your store, and with it your purchase history in Bodyladder: which product, when, the price and currency, and whether a subscription is active, renewing or cancelled. It also receives your device’s operating system, app version and store country, and, as part of any internet connection, your IP address. RevenueCat uses this only to verify purchases and give us aggregate sales reports; it does not use it for advertising and does not track you across apps. This record is not linked to your name or email, because Bodyladder does not have them.

If you never buy, RevenueCat still receives the random identifier and the device details above, but there is no purchase history to record.

Your choices and your rights

Everything you log is in your hands: it stays on your device, and uninstalling Bodyladder removes your local database from it. To stop the anonymous statistics as well, uninstall the app.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Ukraine or California, the law gives you rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection over personal data about you. Your training data is processed on your device only, and the statistics we receive are anonymous and not linked to your identity. The one record that concerns you individually is the purchase record held by RevenueCat: write to us and we will tell you what it holds or delete it. Apple and Google keep their own record of what you bought; you can ask them directly. Where processing is based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working, you may object to it at any time.

Children

Bodyladder is intended for adults and is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Your training data never leaves your device, so its security is the security of your device: a screen lock and device encryption protect it. We hold no copy of it and therefore cannot lose it — and cannot restore it either, which is what the Export file is for.
