Privacy at a Glance

The content you create in the app — meat weights, batch logs, recipes, reminders, and notes — is stored on your device. If you use an optional backup or export feature, this data may be stored through your device's platform account (Google or Apple) under their respective policies.

To operate and improve the app, third-party services used by the app may automatically collect certain technical information, which may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address;
  • Device model and operating system version;
  • App configuration, features used, and time and date of use;
  • Diagnostic and crash information.
