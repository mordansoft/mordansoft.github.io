Privacy Policy

Toneladder is an ear training app developed by MordanSoft. It plays a cadence and a note, you name the scale degree, and it explains the ones you miss. This policy explains what happens to your data — and the short answer is: your training stays on your device, and the only thing that leaves it is anonymous usage statistics and crash reports.

No account, no servers of ours

Toneladder has no accounts and no sign-up, and we run no server that stores your data. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts, and we have no way to identify you personally.

Your training data

Your answers, the level you have reached, your accuracy for each scale degree, your day streak and your settings are stored in a local database on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and they are not sent to our analytics. Toneladder does not use your microphone — it never listens to you, and it asks for no microphone permission. It does not use your camera, your location or your device’s sensors, and it reads nothing from your music library.

Usage statistics and crash reports

Released versions of Toneladder send anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase and crash diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics (both provided by Google). This is the only data that leaves your device, and we use it for one purpose: to see which parts of the app work and which fail, so we can fix them.

What is sent: that something happened in the app (the app was opened for the first time, a first answer was given, a lesson of ten questions was finished, an explanation screen was played back, a level opened, a level stepped back), which level number that was, your device model, operating system version, app version, language and country, and a random app-instance identifier generated on your device. Crash reports add the technical details of the failure.

What is never sent: the answers you gave, which degree you confuse, your accuracy, your journal, your day streak, your settings, your name, email or contacts, your exact location, and any advertising identifier.

Development builds send nothing at all. Google processes this data as our service provider, on servers that may be outside your country, including in the United States, under the transfer safeguards in Google’s own terms. Anonymous event data is retained for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days. We cannot link any of it back to you, and we do not try to.

No advertising, no tracking

Toneladder shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDKs. We do not profile you, we do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not use advertising identifiers, and we never sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers. On iPhone, the app does not ask for permission to track, because it does not.

No purchases in this version

This version of Toneladder is free and contains no purchases at all, so there is no payment, no receipt and no purchase record of any kind. If a purchase is ever added, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app’s release notes will say so.

When the app uses the internet

Only for the statistics and crash reports described above. The sounds, the levels, the explanations and your journal are built into the app and work with no connection at all.

Your choices and your rights

Everything you train is in your hands: it stays on your device, and uninstalling Toneladder removes the local database with it. To stop the anonymous statistics as well, uninstall the app.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Ukraine or California, the law gives you rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection over personal data about you. Your training data is processed on your device only, and the statistics we receive are anonymous and not linked to your identity, so there is no account for us to look up — but if you believe we hold anything about you, write to us and we will tell you what we can find and delete it. Where processing is based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working, you may object to it at any time.

Children

Toneladder is intended for adults and is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Your training data never leaves your device, so its security is the security of your device: a screen lock and device encryption protect it. We hold no copy of it and therefore cannot lose it — and cannot restore it either, which is worth knowing before you change phones.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date below. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
