Points have no monetary value. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred between families, cashed out or exchanged for anything through the app, and we provide no rewards of any kind. What a child receives for points is decided and given by the parent.

The app is a tool, not advice on parenting, education, health or child development. Decisions about rules, rewards, pressure and consequences are yours. Set tasks that are safe, lawful and suitable for your child's age, and do not use the app to punish, shame or coerce a child.
