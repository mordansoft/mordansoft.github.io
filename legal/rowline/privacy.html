Privacy Policy

Rowline is a knitting and crochet companion developed by MordanSoft. It shows the patterns you already own and counts your rows. This policy explains what happens to your data — and the short answer is: your patterns and your projects stay on your device, and what leaves it is anonymous usage statistics and crash reports.

No account, no servers of ours

Rowline has no accounts and no sign-up, and we run no server that stores your content. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts, and we have no way to identify you personally.

Your patterns

The patterns you import — PDF files, photos of a page, or pages you open by link — are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We do not read them, we do not copy them, and we never share them.

Rowline does not sell, host or generate patterns. It works only with the files you bring yourself, and the copyright in those patterns remains with their authors.

Your projects and notes

Projects, counters, notes and progress are stored in a local database on your device. If you use Export, a backup file is created and goes wherever you send it — that file is yours, and it does not pass through us.

Usage statistics and crash reports

Released versions of Rowline send anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase and crash diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics (both provided by Google). This is the only data that leaves your device without you asking for it, and we use it for one purpose: to see which parts of the app work and which fail, so we can fix them.

What is sent: that a screen or step happened (for example: a pattern was imported, a project was created, a row was counted, the paywall was shown, an import failed and why), coarse measures such as how many rows and roughly how many minutes a session lasted, the number of pages in an imported file and whether rows were found in it, your device model, operating system version, app version, language and country, and a random app-instance identifier generated on your device. Crash reports add the technical details of the failure.

What is never sent: the content of your patterns, imported files or photos; file names; project names; your notes; the addresses you import from; your name, email or contacts; your exact location; and any advertising identifier. Counted rows and minutes are sent in ranges, not exact numbers.

Development builds send nothing at all. Google processes this data as our service provider, on servers that may be outside your country, including in the United States, under the transfer safeguards in Google’s own terms. Anonymous event data is retained for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days. We cannot link any of it back to you, and we do not try to.

No advertising, no tracking

Rowline shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDKs. We do not profile you, we do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not use advertising identifiers, and we never sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

When the app uses the internet

Only when you ask it to, plus the statistics described above: if you import a pattern by link, the app downloads that page from the address you entered. No other content requests are made by the app.

Camera and photo library

If you choose to photograph a pattern page or pick an image, the app asks for permission at that moment. The images are used only to build your pattern and are kept on your device. You can decline, and everything else in the app keeps working.

Purchases

The current version of Rowline is free and contains no in-app purchases, so there is no billing data of any kind. If a paid option is added later, it will be sold and charged by Apple or Google under that store’s own terms — we would never see your card details or billing information, only the fact from the store that a purchase was made — and this policy will be updated before it ships.

Your choices and your rights

Everything you create is in your hands: you can delete any project or pattern inside the app, and uninstalling Rowline removes your local database and all imported files from the device. To stop the anonymous statistics as well, uninstall the app — there is nothing of yours left with us afterwards, because we hold no personal data to delete.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Ukraine or California, the law gives you rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection over personal data about you. We process your content on your device only, and the statistics we receive are anonymous and not linked to your identity, so in practice there is no personal data of yours for us to produce or erase. You are welcome to write to us anyway and we will explain what we hold; where processing is based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working, you may object to it at any time.

Children

Rowline is a tool for crafters and is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Your content never leaves your device, so its security is the security of your device: a screen lock and device encryption protect it. We hold no copy of it and therefore cannot lose it — and cannot restore it either, which is what the Export backup is for.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date below. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
