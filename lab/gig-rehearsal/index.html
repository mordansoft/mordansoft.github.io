1112About this gig

Your app has installs and some revenue, but you are deciding what to build next by gut feel?

I am a senior Flutter developer with 7+ years in software, and I run my own subscription app on both stores: 50,000+ installs. Every pricing, paywall and retention decision I made there came out of a data pipeline I built for it. I point the same pipeline at your app.

What you get:

  • Your numbers pulled out of Google Play Console, App Store Connect and your subscription service into one clean dataset
  • A written report: where installs, trials and paying users drop off, and what each drop costs you per month
  • A ranked list of concrete actions, most valuable first, with the reasoning behind the order
  • Premium: the export script plus questions to ask your own AI assistant, so you can keep doing this without me

Honest terms: I work with the data you already have. No events instrumented? We work from store and subscription data, and I will tell you exactly what to add. I report what the numbers say. I do not promise growth: that depends on what you do with the findings.

Send me your app link and tell me which decision you are stuck on.

16FAQ

I do not have analytics set up at all. Can you still help?

That is the common case, and Basic still works: your store consoles and subscription service already hold installs, conversion, churn, pricing and refunds. You get the report plus a short list of what is worth instrumenting next.

Why can I not just read the dashboards myself?

Dashboards answer the questions you already thought to ask, and they do not join up: Play Console does not know what App Store Connect or your paywall knows. The work here is putting them in one place and reading them against each other.

Can you implement the fixes too?

Yes, as a custom offer once we both know what is worth doing. The report stands on its own either way, and I will say plainly which items you can do yourself.
