// What it does

Built around the pattern in your hands

Your own patterns, any source

Import a PDF, photograph a page from a book, or open a pattern you found on the web. Rowline does not sell, host or generate patterns — it works with what you already have, and the copyright stays with the author.

A highlight bar on the row you are on

The bar covers the whole row you are working, not a thin line under it — horizontal for written patterns, vertical for charts. Tap anywhere to count a row, hold to go back.

Counters and notes that fit real projects

A main row counter plus linked counters for repeats and increases, and notes that appear exactly when you reach the row you wrote them on.

Your patterns stay yours

Nothing is uploaded. No account, no servers of ours, no advertising. The app reaches the network only when you ask it to open a pattern by link.
