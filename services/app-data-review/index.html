// FAQ

Questions

I don't have analytics set up at all. Can you still help?

That's the common case and Basic still works: your store consoles and subscription service already hold installs, conversion, churn, pricing and refunds. You get the report plus a short list of what's worth instrumenting next.

Why can't I just read the dashboards myself?

Dashboards answer the questions you already thought to ask, and they don't join: Play Console doesn't know what App Store Connect or your paywall knows. The work here is putting them in one place and reading them against each other.

Can you implement the fixes too?

Yes, as a custom offer once we both know what is worth doing. The report stands on its own either way, and I'll say plainly which items you can do yourself.
