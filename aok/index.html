What it does

AoK turns your phone into a real angle meter for knife sharpening. Lay it on the blade — get the exact angle. No guesswork, no ruined edges.

  • Live angle meter, updated in real time
  • Whetstone guide — audio beep when the blade hits the target angle
  • Works with any tool — whetstone, guided rod, belt sharpener, bench grinder
  • Blade log — save every knife with angle, steel, photo, and full sharpening history
  • Presets for chef's, pocket, hunting, kitchen utility, fruit & veg knives, axe, and more
  • 8 languages, metric and imperial
