Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Toneladder has no sign-up, no ads and no purchases. Your levels, answers and journal stay on your device and work offline.

I hear nothing

Tap I hear nothing during a lesson, or Check sound in Settings: it plays a test note and walks through the usual causes — volume, silent mode, and headphones that are connected to something else. Toneladder plays through the normal media output, so it is not muted by silent mode; if your headphones are paired but playing nowhere, disconnect and reconnect them and run Check sound again.

The lesson stopped on its own

That is deliberate. If you unplug or disconnect your headphones mid-lesson, the lesson pauses instead of playing out loud, and a phone call pauses it too. Tap to continue and you are back on the same question.

Why does it play a chord before every question?

That is the cadence, and it is the point. A note on its own is not a scale degree — it becomes one inside a key. The cadence puts the key in your ear so the note has somewhere to sit. This is what separates ear training by degree from guessing notes.

How do I move to the next level?

A level opens when 18 of your last 20 answers on it are right. If you drop below five right answers in a single lesson, you step back one level — nothing in your history is lost, and you can walk back up the same way.

Do you teach absolute (perfect) pitch?

No, and we do not promise it. Toneladder trains relative pitch: hearing where a note sits inside a key. Naming notes with no reference is a different skill, it is not what the app does, and the app says so in Settings.

Can I answer with numbers instead of Do Re Mi?

Yes — Settings → Answer labels → 1 · 2 · 3. Settings also holds the key: always C, or a new key for each lesson.

Does it work with a screen reader?

Yes. Every button, answer and result is labelled for VoiceOver and TalkBack. If you find a control that reads wrong or reads nothing, write to us — for an app that works by ear, that is a bug we want to hear about first.

Where is my progress stored? Can I move it to a new phone?

On your device only. There is no account and no sync in this version, so a new phone starts fresh. Deleting the app deletes the data with it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app — everything stored on the device goes with it. We hold no account and no copy; what the app sends is anonymous statistics that cannot be traced back to you. Questions: mordansoft@gmail.com.

Is it bad for my ears?

Keep the volume comfortable, especially in headphones. Loud listening over long sessions can damage hearing; a lesson is about five minutes and does not need to be loud. Toneladder is not a hearing test and not medical advice — if you think something is wrong with your hearing, see a professional.
